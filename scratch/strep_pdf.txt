Streptococcus A Lab-On-Time™  Test Device (BMT 51084) 
CLINICAL VALIDATION REPORT 
 
 
1. OBJECTIVE 
The purpose of the test is to validate the performance of BMT’s Streptococcus A Lab -On-Time™  Test 
Device and evaluate the usability of this rapid test in the hands of professional users and their ability to 
properly perform it based on the provided IFU and to correctly interpret its result. 
 
2. OVERVIEW 
Streptococcus pyogenes (Strep A) is non-motile gram-positive cocci, which contains the Lancefield group 
A antigens that can cause serious infections such as pharyngitis, respiratory infection, impetigo,  
endocarditis, meningitis, puerperal sepsis, and arthritis.  
Pharyngitis (strep throat) is a common infectious disease among 5–15-year-old and characterized by the 
rapid appearance of symptoms including: headache, fever, throat and swallowing pain and stomachache. 
Throat inspection usually reveals erythema at the ba ck of the throat and the larynx with white effluent, 
swollen tonsils and sometimes also painful and enlarged neck lymph nodes. Left untreated, strep A 
infections may lead to serious complications, including rheumatic fever and peritonsillar abscess. T he 
traditional identification procedures for Group A Streptococci infection involve the isolation and 
identification of viable organisms using techniques that require 24 to 48 hours or longer.  
The Strep Lab-on-TimeTM Strep A Rapid Test is an integrated device for throat swab sample collection and 
analysis, employing a qualitative lateral flow immunoassay for the rapid (5-10min) detection of Strep A 
carbohydrate antigen. In this test, antibody specific to Strep A carbohydrate antigen is coated on the test 
line region of the test. During testing, the extracted throat swab specimen reacts with an antibody to 
Strep A that is coated onto  gold nano particles. The mixture migrates by capillary forces along the 
membrane to react with the antibody to Strep A on the membrane and generate a colored line in the test 
line region. The presence of this color ed line in the test line region indicates a positive result, while its 
absence indicates a negative result. To serve as a procedural control, a colored line will always appear in 
the control li ne region, indicating that  proper volume of specimen has been added and membrane 
wicking has occurred.  This test is qualitative, displaying an analytical sensitivity (limit of detection) of 
1*10^5 Strep A organisms. It is not intended for the quantitative determination of Strep A bacteria. 
Use of this device in the hospital, clinic, or even self -use by the patient will provide a rapid result 
indicating the need for antibiotic treatment (such as by Penicillin and its derivatives) and may eliminate 
the need for complicated, time-consuming, and expensive tests, such as PCR or microbiological culture.

• Directly compare between the results obtained using the Strep A Lab-On-Time™ Rapid Test Device 
with freshly obtained throat swab samples from symptomatic patients and results obtained with

4. VALIDATION STUDY OUTLINE  
The test was validated at the Tzafon Medical Center, Poriya, Israel in Feb-Mar 2023 under the supervision 
of Prof. Avi Peretz (PI) using kits provided by BMT Diagnostics (BMT 51084, LOT , Expiry ).  
Study recruitment was performed using a Consent Form that was signed by the patients. All data 
regarding the patients was encoded and available only to the PI. Age of recruited patents was over 3 
years, and the study was open to both women and man. Excluded from this study were individuals who 
lack judgement or are employees/students supervised by the PI. Also excluded are patients who have 
received antibiotics of any kind 24 (or less) prior to test performance.  
The study was conducted on 181 patients, of which 70 were positive and 111 negatives to Strep A 
according to the microbiological culture test. The test was performed according to the test 
manufacturer's protocol (IFU). The comparison was made using duplicate patient samples sent to the 
microbiological lab for culture and analysis. 
The test was performed by a lab technician, simulating self-testing conditions by collecting a throat sample 
from the patient by the device swab and following the protocol provided in the IFU. A matching throat swab 
specimen was collected and used for microbiological validation of the results. 
The lab technician didn’t have any previous experience with performing this test and was therefore only 
exposed to and educated by the IFU. This assured that the technician operated the test as a lay person 
would, thereby simulating self-testing. 
The following information was collected for each recruited patient: test results (irrespective of the 
current study), age and sex, and how long prior to the test has the patient observed fever and throat 
pain. Test validation results will be reported to the Israeli Ministry of Health according to the clinical 
validation protocol, including the observed test sensitivity and specificity.

The Strep A Lab-On-Time device is indicated for professional use by a trained medical practitioner 
(nurse/physician) in the physician’s office or in the lab. The test is performed by extracting the device and 
reagent tube from their respective aluminum pou ches, followed by removing the device cap, collecting 
the throat sample using the swab end of the device and transferring the device into the reagent tube 
such that the swab end of the device is being soaked in the tube reagent. The device end is then mixe d 
in the tube for around 10 seconds, followed by reading the test results in the device’s results window 
(see also product IFU in the Appendix for additional detail).

7. OBSERVATIONS AND CONCLUSION 
The above evaluation study compared the diagnostic test results obtained using BMT Diagnostics Lab -
=== PAGE 4 ===
4 
 
 
On-Time Strep A Rapid Test Device with culture test results obtained by the microbiological laboratory 
using standard culture techniques performed using 181 throat swab samples obtained from healthy (111) 
and strep A patients. From this study, it is concluded that the specificity of BMT’s rapid test is 100%, 
whereas the overall sensitivity was found to be 88.6%. 
Further analysis performed according to the number of strep A colonies appearing following 
microbiological culture demonstrated that when considering only colony -rich bacterial culture, the 
sensitivity of BMT’s rapid test increased to 96.9%. 
BMT’s rapid test device has been shown to be very user friendly. Test performance is very intuitive and 
involves only a single step of sample collection and placement of the test device in the provided, pre -
filled reagent tube. Test results were clear and obtained in less than 10 min following throat sample 
collection by the device’s swab. 
It is my professional opinion that BMT Diagnostics Lab -On-Time Strep A Rapid Test Device is very similar 
in its results to those obtained by bacterial culture and could be easily adopted by professional users due 
to its simplicity, ease of use, and clear results reading.
