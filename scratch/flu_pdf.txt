Influenza A+B Lab-On-Time™ Test Device (BMT 51088) 
CLINICAL VALIDATION REPORT 
 
The purpose of the test is to validate the performance of BMT’s Influenza A+B Lab-On-Time™ Test Device 
and evaluate the usability of this rapid test in the hands of untrained users and their ability to properly 
perform it based on the provided IFU and to correctly interpret its result. 
 
1. OBJECTIVES 
• Direct comparison between the results obtained using the Influenza A+B  Rapid Test 
Device with freshly obtained patient nasal samples and results obtained with matching 
samples using a lab-based PCR test (the TaqManTM SARS-CoV-2, Flu A, Flu B Multiplex 
Assay (AppliedBiosystems, Waltham, MA, USA)). 
• Evaluate the usability of BMT’s Influenza A+B Lab -On-Time™ Test Device  for the purpose of 
recommending this device for self-testing in the hands of lay untrained users. 
 
2. PERFORMANCE OF TESTING 
The test was validated at the Tzafon Medical Center, Poriya, Israel in Feb-Mar 2023 under the supervision 
of Prof. Avi Peretz. The test was performed according to the test manufacturer's protocol (IFU). The 
comparison was made with the TaqManTM SARS-CoV-2, Flu A, Flu B Multiplex Assay (AppliedBiosystems) 
PCR kit in the lab using the QuantStudio™ 5 Real-Time PCR System (Thermo Scientific, Waltham, MA, 
USA). 
The test was performed by a lab technician, simulating self-testing conditions by collecting a nasal sample 
from the patient’s nose and following the protocol provided in the IFU. A matching nasal specimen was 
collected and used for PCR validation of the results. 
The lab technician didn’t have any previous experience with performing this test and was therefore only 
exposed to and educated by the IFU. This assured that the technician operated the test as a lay person 
would, thereby simulating self-testing.

4. OBSERVATIONS AND CONCLUSION 
The above evaluation study compared the diagnostic test results obtained using BMT Diagnostics Lab -
On-Time Influenza A+B Rapid Test Device with the diagnostic test results obtained with a PCR test (the 
TaqManTM SARS-CoV-2, Flu A, Flu B Multiplex Assay (performed in the lab on 170 nasal samples obtained 
from healthy (100) and inf luenza patients. From this study, it is concluded that the specificity of BMT’s 
rapid test is 100%, whereas the overall sensitivity was found to be 75.7%. 
Further analysis performed according to PCR CT values obtained for nasal samples from influenza patients 
demonstrated that when considering only CT cutoff values of <35 the overall sensitivity of BMT’s rapid 
test increased to 84.1%. 
Furthermore, full correlation was observed between influenza types (A or B) determined by the PCR test 
and the results obtained with BMT’s rapid test.

Finally, BMT’s rapid test device has been shown to be very user friendly. Test performance is very intuitive 
and involves only a single step of sample collection and placement of the test device in the provided, pre-
filled reagent tub e. Test results were clear and obtained in less than 10 min following nasal sample 
collection by the device. 
It is my professional opinion that BMT Diagnostics Lab-On-Time Influenza A+B Rapid Test Device could be 
used by the general population as a self-test due to its simplicity, ease of use, and clear results reading.
